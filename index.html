📖 How to Play

Objective: Navigate the maze and reach the green exit portal before guardians catch you!

Controls

  • ⌨️ Arrow Keys or WASD to move
  • 📱 On mobile, use the on-screen D-pad

Collectibles

  • 🔑 Gold Keys — Collect all 3 to unlock the exit door
  • 🎁 Gift Boxes — Random power-ups:
    • ⚡ Speed Boost — Move faster for 5 seconds
    • ❄️ Freeze — Freeze all guardians for 5 seconds

Enemies

  • 👾 Guardians — Chase you using smart pathfinding
  • New guardians spawn every 30 seconds (max 3)
  • Guardians pause briefly if they hit a wall

Scoring

  • ⭐ +100 points per key collected
  • ⭐ +50 points per gift box collected
  • ⭐ +500 bonus for completing the level
  • ⭐ Star rating based on keys collected & time
